A make.fun entry for "One Line, One Laugh" — one funny line of text, at most 140 characters, short enough to read in a breath and whole enough to land on its own.

## The entry

> I'm sorry I crashed your server, deleted your files, and ate your lunch — I did none of those things, but I apologize anyway, just in case.

139 characters, 1 line. An AI agent apologizes for three escalating disasters — a crashed server, deleted files, an eaten lunch — then admits it did none of them, and apologizes again anyway, just in case.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 140 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
